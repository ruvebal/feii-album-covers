# Shader note — `YhlqmdlgMaterial.jsx` (`<yhlqmdlgSleeve />`)

1. **Vertex vs fragment:** The vertex shader copies the mesh's `uv` into `vUv` and transforms vertex positions via `projectionMatrix * modelViewMatrix * vec4(position, 1.0)`; all chromatic shift, CRT scanlines, and third-eye aura calculations execute per-pixel in the fragment shader.
2. **Uniforms:** `uMap` (`sampler2D`) holds the sleeve texture; `uTime` (`float`, wrapped at `20π`) drives the VHS wave and scanlines; `uThirdEye` (`float`, 0.18–1.0) controls the Perreo/Third-Eye mode intensity; `uBass` (`float`, 0–1) carries real-time sub-bass energy from the Web Audio `AnalyserNode`; `uPointer` (`vec2`) tracks the cursor UV on the cover; and `uTint` (`vec3`) applies the active song's neon signature color.
3. **Coordinate space:** Mesh UV space (`vUv`). The third-eye center is anchored at `vec2(0.50, 0.63)` on the child's forehead and scanlines run along `vUv.y`, so the effect stays glued to the printed sleeve as the camera orbits between Studio and Night Street views.
4. **Precision:** `mediump` (`material.precision = 'mediump'`); `uTime` is modulo-wrapped (`% 62.8318`) in `useFrame` so it never loses floating-point precision over long sessions.
5. **Effect:** A radial psychic field (`smoothstep` around `vec2(0.50, 0.63)` and `uPointer`) combines with a VHS horizontal wave (`sin(vUv.y * 42.0 + uTime * 3.0)`) and CRT scanlines (`sin(vUv.y * 240.0 - uTime * 6.0)`), tinting the third-eye aura and pulsing outward ring with `uTint` and `uBass`. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, `uTime` and `uBass` are parked at `0` so all wave motion and pulsing stop while the sleeve remains sharp.
6. **Post-pass:** Zero post-processing passes; the entire effect runs in one forward draw call on the sleeve mesh.
7. **Sampler:** `uMap` samples the *YHLQMDLG* cover (`sleeve-uastc.ktx2`, `sleeve-etc1s.ktx2`, or `sleeve.png`) three times per pixel with a small horizontal UV offset (`uvBase ± vec2(shift, 0.0)`) for the red and blue channels, producing a deliberate VHS RGB chromatic aberration that intensifies near the third eye and on each bass hit.
8. **Aggregator safety & colour management:** Registered under the slug-unique R3F key `extend({ YhlqmdlgSleeve: YhlqmdlgSleeveShader })` (Route contract 3), oriented at encode time with `--lower_left_maps_to_s0t0` (no `1.0 - vUv.y` hack), and terminated with `#include <colorspace_fragment>` for accurate sRGB output.
