Two criteria, explained

Merge judgement

Your ai-3d-merge-log.md records each assistant suggestion you kept, refused or escalated, with the gate and the reason. Two real refusals is the floor. A log of only accepts shows compliance, not judgement.

Process discipline

How the work reaches main: small staged PRs, task1 before task2 before task3, the branch pattern, the template filled in, one registry line. A reviewer should read each PR in one sitting.

A route should be as small as its idea

  • One file past five hundred lines
  • State the reader cannot hold
  • A patch that hides a broken asset
  • A log the assistant wrote for you
